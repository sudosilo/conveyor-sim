Conveyor Sim

Conveyor Sim shows the pieces of a software pipeline as physical machines, with condiment packets as the data. A sorter is a router, a counter bin is a cache, a filing cabinet is a database, a half rack is reassembly, and a fabricator is what a website finally makes. Packets come from the Packet Baron game: 160 sauces, 7 wrapper styles and 50/50 packets, over 12,600 variations.

Open an example, or turn on Build to place machines, connect them with belts and watch the packets run. Your build is kept in the browser and the Share tab copies a link that holds the whole plan. Static site, flat isometric view, light theme.

Live at conveyor-sim.vercel.app
